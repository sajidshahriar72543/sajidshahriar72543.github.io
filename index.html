About me

I am a researcher and lecturer with interests in machine learning, EEG signal processing, computer vision, and privacy-preserving AI. I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology, Bangladesh, and currently serve as a Lecturer at the European University of Bangladesh. Alongside teaching, I work as a Research Assistant at Transparent Intelligence Lab, focusing on medical imaging and intelligent healthcare systems.

My research focuses on deep learning for EEG analysis, federated learning, and lightweight AI architectures for real-world applications. I am passionate about building efficient and deployable AI solutions, mentoring students, and contributing to open-source development and collaborative research projects.
