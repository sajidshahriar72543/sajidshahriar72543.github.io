About

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at the European University of Bangladesh. I also work as a Research Assistant at the Transparent Intelligence Lab at RUET, with Dr. Md Nahiduzzaman, and at the ELITE Research Lab, with Md Kishor Morol. I hold a B.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology.

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical signal processing. I design lightweight, interpretable architectures for EEG analysis particularly brain-computer interfaces, and study how well they generalize to unseen subjects. I have also worked on privacy-preserving federated learning for decentralized healthcare and on computer vision for remote sensing. A recurring result across these projects is that simpler methods often generalize better when data is scarce: my current work on cross-subject EEG locomotion decoding shows classical spatial filtering outperforming deep models under artifact-aware, subject-wise evaluation. Long term, I want to contribute to clinical AI systems reliable enough to be used outside controlled experimental settings.
